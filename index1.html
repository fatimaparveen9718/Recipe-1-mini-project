Chocolate Cake Recipe

Chocolate cake is a delicious and soft dessert. It is perfect for birthdays and special occasions.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 2 tablespoons cocoa powder
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder

Instructions

  1. Mix flour, sugar, cocoa powder, and baking powder.
  2. Add milk and oil, then mix well.
  3. Pour the mixture into a cake pan.
  4. Bake until the cake is fully cooked.

Important: Mix all ingredients well.

Enjoy your homemade chocolate cake.
